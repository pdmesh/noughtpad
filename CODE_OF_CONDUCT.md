# Code of conduct

Noughtpad is a small project, and we want it to be a good place to ask questions, report problems and contribute.

- Be kind and assume good intent. Disagree about ideas, not people.
- No harassment, insults, or personal attacks, and nothing sexual, hateful or threatening.
- Don't publish anyone's private information.
- Keep discussion on topic, and respect a maintainer's decision to close or lock a thread.

If someone breaks these rules, tell us through [noughtpad.com/contact](https://noughtpad.com/contact/). Reports are read only by the maintainer and kept confidential. We may edit or remove comments, close issues, or block people from the project.
