The Journal shows your own notes in date order, like a diary. Notes with a source (things you read or heard) are left out, so it stays about your own thinking.

## Days, weeks and months

Click **Journal** in the sidebar. Use the **Days**, **Weeks** and **Months** switch at the top right to change how notes are grouped. Click any note to open it.

## The activity map

At the top of the Journal is a year of your writing, one square per day. The darker the square, the more you wrote that day. Hover over a square to see the date and number of notes. The shades adjust to your own habits, so a busy day for you shows dark whatever "busy" means for you.

Above it are four numbers:

- **Notes this year**
- **Active days:** days you wrote at least one note.
- **Day streak:** days in a row up to today.
- **Longest streak** this year.

These are there to look at. Noughtpad doesn't set goals, send reminders or give badges, and a broken streak doesn't mean anything.
