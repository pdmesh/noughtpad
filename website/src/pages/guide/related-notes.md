Related notes shows you other notes that seem connected to the one you're reading. It's a good way to rediscover something you wrote months ago and forgot.

## Turn it on

Related notes needs the small model used for search by meaning. If you haven't turned it on yet, open **Settings** and turn on **Search by meaning, related notes and ideas over time** under **Finding connections**. The panel also offers a **Turn on** button the first time.

## Open it

With a note open, press **⌘J** (Ctrl+J), or click the related-notes button at the top right. A panel opens on the right. Press **Esc** or **⌘J** again to close it.

It never opens by itself and never shows anything while you type.

## What each suggestion shows

- **How sure it is.** "These ideas appear connected" for a strong match, "Possibly related" for a weaker one.
- **The passage that matched**, and the passage in your note it matched.
- **Labels the notes share**, or the **same source**.
- **How far apart in time** they were written, for example "7 months earlier".

Click a suggestion to open that note. If the note is part of a thread in [Ideas over time](/guide/ideas-over-time/), a link at the top takes you to it.

## Hide a wrong suggestion

Click **Not related** on a suggestion. It disappears for good, in both directions: neither note will suggest the other again. An **Undo** appears for a few seconds in case you clicked by mistake.

## It learns what you find useful

Noughtpad notices which suggestions you open and which you mark "Not related". After a handful of each, it starts using that to decide what to show. The panel then says "Tuned to what you open and dismiss here".

To start over, open **Settings** and click **Reset** next to **Reset what related notes learned**. Notes you've marked "Not related" stay hidden.

## Ask about a note

At the bottom of the panel, **Ask about this note…** starts a chat about the open note and its related notes. [About chat](/guide/chat-and-models/)
