Open Settings from the bottom of the sidebar, with **⌘,** on a Mac, or by typing "settings" in the **⌘K** box.

## Notes folder

Shows where your notes are. **Switch…** opens a different folder. Your old folder stays as it was; you can switch back at any time.

**Rebuild the search index** rebuilds Noughtpad's index from your notes. You only need it if search ever seems out of date. Your notes aren't touched.

## Sync

Sign in with Google and turn on **Sync this notebook**. Shows when the last sync happened, and anything that needs your attention. [About sync](/guide/sync/)

## Finding connections

**Search by meaning, related notes and ideas over time.** Turns on the small model (about 65 MB) that runs on your computer and powers all three. You can turn it off again.

**Reset what related notes learned.** Related notes adjust to what you open and dismiss. This forgets that and starts fresh. Notes you've marked "Not related" stay hidden.

## Chat

**Language models** shows which model chat uses, if any, and takes you to the Models page. [About chat and models](/guide/chat-and-models/)

## Writing

- **Font** for note text: System (your computer's own font), Serif, Rounded, Monospace, or any font installed on your computer under **Other…**.
- **Text size**: System, or a size from 12 to 28 pixels.

A sample line shows the result. These apply to this computer only.

## Layout

- **Always show the sidebar and toolbar.** Otherwise they stay hidden until you move the pointer to the edge. Same as **⌘\\**.
- **Show recent notes under the editor.** The writing page is a blank page by default; this adds a list of recent notes below it.

## Start-up

**Open at login** starts Noughtpad when you log in to your computer, so quick capture (**⌘⇧Space**) works straight away.

- On a Mac it starts in the background. Closing the window keeps it running; **⌘Q** quits.
- On Windows and Linux it starts minimised. Closing the window quits.
